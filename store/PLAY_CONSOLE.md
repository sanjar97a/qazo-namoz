## 7. Yopiq test (Closed testing)
1. Testing → Closed testing → Create track → **namoz-qazo-play.aab** ni yuklang.
2. Testers: **Google Groups** yoki email ro‘yxati — kamida **12 kishi** (ehtiyot uchun 15–20).
3. Testerlarga “Join on the web” havolasini yuboring: ular qo‘shilib, ilovani Play’dan o‘rnatadi va **14 kun ketma-ket** o‘chirmaydi.
4. 14 kundan keyin: Dashboard → **Apply for production**.

## 8. Play App Signing
Birinchi .aab yuklanganda **“Use Google-generated key”** ni tanlang (tavsiya). Bizning `upload.keystore` faqat yuklash kaliti. U yo‘qolsa, Play Console orqali yangisini so‘rash mumkin.
